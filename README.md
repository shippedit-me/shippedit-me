### 🚀 What is shippedit?

Not just another launch directory. shippedit is where you **launch once and keep growing** — your product gets a permanent home, real distribution, and a weekly system that keeps working long after launch day ends.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🗓️ **Weekly launch battle** | Launch Monday → Sunday, the community votes, the best launch wins the week. Weighted scoring and a 48-hour reveal keep it fair. |
| 🏠 **Permanent page** | A real product page that ranks on Google, with a **dofollow backlink** — yours forever. |
| 📈 **Real analytics** | Impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions — plus a Monday digest of how your launch did. |
| 🔍 **Automated audits** | Page speed, security & Safe Browsing, and an **AI-readiness (AEO)** score for how visible you are to ChatGPT and Perplexity. |
| ⚡ **AI fix prompts** | Failing an audit? Get the what, the why, and a paste-ready prompt for Cursor or Claude Code. |
| 👀 **Competitor monitoring** | Watch competitor pages for changes, with alerts the moment something ships. |
| 👤 **Founder profile** | Your own audience, your reputation — it compounds across every launch you ship. |
